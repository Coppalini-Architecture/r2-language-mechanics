# Handoff Protocol

Each material handoff records:

- **Source** — immutable reference, path, or supplied input
- **Reader** — declared reader and resolution
- **Claim** — `exact`, `construction`, or `open`
- **Check** — repeatable command or stated manual procedure
- **Receipt** — output, hash, or observation
- **Delta** — witnessed change from the preceding record
- **Open** — residual obligation

A receipt preserves what was checked. It does not enlarge the claim beyond the declared reader and check.
