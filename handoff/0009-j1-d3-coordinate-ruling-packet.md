- **Open** — The ruling, typed. For a file F with raw bytes `B_F` and I1 defect set `D`, I1 reports `F:ℓ` with `ℓ` a raw LF line number (line k begins after the (k−1)-th LF; a CR does not end a line, per 0007). Three decisions are open. (a) The coordinate of one defect. CR: the line of the first CR (both readers; `lab_cr_l40`, `self_cr_l20`, `chain_cr_l7`). Missing terminal LF: the unterminated last line, `LF + 1` (both). Extra terminal LFs, k of them: `P1`, the line of the first extra LF, `LF − k + 1`, which for k = 1 is the blank line; or `P2`, the last blank line, `LF` (both readers today, for `lab.txt` and, in B, for `CHAIN.txt`). They separate on `lab_xLF2` (173 against 174) and `chain_xLF2` (21 against 22); for `chain_xLF2`, `P1` also agrees with the I8 coordinate. (b) Several defects in one file. `Q1`: the minimum offset-line over `D`. `Q2`: the first defect in a fixed kind order, then that defect's coordinate (both readers on this mutant). They separate on `self_nfd23_cr40` (23 against 40). The two readers order CR and invalid UTF-8 differently (A: CR first; B: UTF-8 first); no mutant here covers that, and UTF-8 failures stay outside the 0008 ruling. (c) `CHAIN.txt`. `R1`: the same coordinate rule as `lab.txt` and `self.txt` (B). `R2`: line 1 for any `CHAIN.txt` I1 failure (A). Every `chain_*` mutant separates them. Separate items, not part of D3: (1) I8 row coverage when the final `CHAIN.txt` row has no LF (receipt 5): what counts as a row under `V(CHAIN)`; A skips such a row. (2) D4 follows once (a) to (c) are ruled. (3) UTF-8 decoding failures. (4) The 0004 policy readings in `j1_check2.py`, the J-09 choices and the address adapters are untouched. (5) For v0.1.1 the spec text needs the chosen coordinate rule in J-04.
